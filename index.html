Suggested content flows

Three ways to structure your video

Pick the opening that fits your style — every flow ends with the same offer and shopping CTA.

01

Visual Results First

Open with the outcome — the flat-tummy visual does the talking before you say a word.

Structure

Flat-tummy visual→ Bloating / irregularity / cravings→ Bundle intro→ Product roles→ Daily routine→ Offer + CTA
  1. Open with a flat-tummy visual, waistband shot, or genuine before-and-after bloating comparison.
  2. Make it seasonal: build your content around Thanksgiving dinners, Christmas parties, or holiday treats. Share your experience with bloating, irregular bathroom habits, or extra snacking during the holiday season.
  3. Introduce the bundle — a simple routine that supports regularity and appetite management.“My daily duo for a less-bloated belly and support with appetite and weight-management goals.”
  4. Explain each role: Daily Fiber Mix helps ease bloating, supports regular bowel movements, and promotes gut health — helping you feel less backed up for a flatter-looking tummy. Metabolic Probiotic supports appetite control and craving management to help you manage food intake and support your midsection weight-management goals.
  5. Show your routine: mix Fiber into a drink you already enjoy and take Metabolic as directed.
  6. Close with the bundle discount, the limited free tote bag offer, and your shopping CTA.
Seasonal angle: pair the visual with a holiday moment — Thanksgiving dinner, Christmas party, or holiday treats — to make the bloating story feel timely.

02

Pain Point First

Start with the feeling everyone recognizes — bloated, backed up, puffy — then offer the duo as the fix.

Structure

Pain point→ Bundle intro→ Product roles→ Daily routine→ Offer + CTA
  1. Open with a relatable pain point: bloating, feeling backed up, or puffiness.“Feeling backed up and bloated after all those holiday meals?”
  2. Introduce the bundle as a simple routine that supports regularity and appetite management.
  3. Explain each product’s role: Daily Fiber Mix supports regular bowel movements to help ease that backed-up, bloated feeling while promoting gut health. Metabolic Probiotic supports appetite control, cravings, and weight-management goals — including concerns around the midsection.
  4. Demonstrate your routine: mix Fiber into your drink, take Metabolic as part of your day.
  5. Share the bundle discount, the limited free tote bag offer, and your CTA.
Seasonal angle: tie the problem to Thanksgiving dinners, Christmas parties, or changes in your holiday routine — then let the duo be the reset.

03

Offer First

Lead with the deal, connect to the problem, and close with a gift-ready CTA.

Structure

Black Friday offer→ Bloating / irregularity / cravings→ Bundle intro→ Product roles→ Daily routine→ CTA
  1. Lead with the Black Friday discount and the limited free tote bag offer.
  2. Connect to a relatable problem.“Feeling backed up and bloated — and constantly reaching for snacks?”
  3. Try a holiday gifting angle: if a friend or family member has shared these concerns — or already loves gut-health products — position the bundle as a thoughtful gift.“Have someone on your holiday list who would love an upgrade to their daily wellness routine?”
  4. Introduce the duo: Daily Fiber Mix helps ease bloating, supports regular bowel movements, and promotes gut health. Metabolic Probiotic supports appetite control, craving management, and weight-management goals.
  5. Show yourself mixing Fiber into your favorite drink and incorporating Metabolic into your daily routine.
  6. Finish with a CTA.“Shop the holiday offer for your own routine — or gift it to someone who’s been wanting to try it.”
Gifting angle: the bundle doubles as a holiday gift — point it at someone on their list who loves gut-health products.
